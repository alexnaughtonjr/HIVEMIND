Murmur

Eight rings

Lead a starling murmuration over a reed marsh. Thread the amber rings, catch the gold columns of warm air, and reach the roost oak before the sun sets.

  • A D or the arrows bank. W S climb and dive. Drag the stick, or drag on the marsh.
  • Fly through each amber ring. A ring folds four more starlings into the flock. A gold column gives daylight.
  • Dark posts with a red ribbon snag birds. A marsh harrier patrols each red line and rakes the flock. Space or Call pulls the flock tight for a short slip.
  • Reach the oak with starlings still beside you. P pauses. R starts over.
