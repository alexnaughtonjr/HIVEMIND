Bramble

Five rings

Roll a hedgehog through a golden-hour orchard. Apples stick to your spines. Shake them through the wicker rings, hop the briars, and reach the burrow before the sun sets.

  • A D or the arrows steer. W speeds up. S slows down. Drag the stick, or drag the orchard.
  • Roll over apples and they stick. A gold apple scores more when you bank it, and it gives a little daylight back.
  • A glowing ring banks every apple on your spines. Four or more at once pays a burst bonus.
  • Dark briars knock apples loose. Space or Hop jumps them. Reach the warm burrow before the sun meter empties.
  • P pauses. R starts over. M mutes.
