Fathom

Five zones

Pilot a research lamp down a trench. Log the animal on the field card in each ocean zone before the lamp goes dark.

  • A D or the arrows slide. W S move in and out. On a touch screen, drag the stick or the trench.
  • The glowing ring is the animal on the card. Steer into it to file the zone.
  • Other animals and rock spires drain the lamp. From the midnight zone down, a hot vent pushes you sideways.
  • Five logs complete the survey. Leftover lamp charge becomes bonus points. P pauses. R starts over.
